About Imazica

Transform kids’ spaces with premium designer acrylic laminates. For the first time in India, Premier introduces an exclusive range made for children’s rooms.

Traditional wallpapers fade, peel or lose their charm. Imazica acrylic laminates are a premium, durable alternative: vibrant colours, crystal-clear printing and a high-gloss finish, in a convenient 4 ft × 8 ft panel.

From cartoon characters and superheroes to space adventures, animals, sports, gaming, educational themes and fantasy worlds, the collection is made to inspire creativity, learning and happiness in every child.

Our mission is to create spaces where children can dream, explore, learn and grow. Bring imagination to your walls with India’s newest generation of designer acrylic laminates.
